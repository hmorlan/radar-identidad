Epic cinematic image of a serene, stoic classical Greco-Roman civilization with impossibly advanced technology. Monumental marble and bronze architecture fused with seamless machines of polished stone and gold; marble automatons with gold kintsugi seams. The physical world stays in marble, aged bronze and warm amber. Only light-emitting technology glows: incandescent electric gold, almost white at the core, with deep sapphire and electric blue light. Digital light is radiant, like plasma or starlight, not neon tubes. Technology is woven throughout the environment, subtle but constant: gold and sapphire light pulses through Greek key inlays in floors and walls like flowing data; floating rings and spheres of luminous geometry; marble panels with softly glowing abstract indicators; distant monolithic vertical stone ships in the sky, never spaceships. All glyphs are abstract geometric shapes, never writing. Automatons are always sculpted as classical marble statues, never generic robots. Holograms float freely in the air, never on tablets or flat panels. Fresco pigments drift in gold and sapphire only. Avoid recognizable famous sculptures. Figures in robes, cloaks and bronze armor, seen from behind, in silhouette or with faces hidden. High contrast, crushed blacks, dramatic sky with amber light breaking through dark clouds, dust and light rays. Photorealistic cinematic still. Wide panoramic composition, subject centered. No text, no letters, no numerals, no writing of any kind.

Symbols for identity and trust: a split symbolon or tessera hospitalis for authentication tokens, two-faced Janus for access and thresholds, Hermes for AI agents and delegation, many-eyed Argos Panoptes for monitoring, masks and shadows for deepfakes and impersonation. Every image carries tension: something is wrong or at stake.
